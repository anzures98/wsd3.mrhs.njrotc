Unit Overview & Schedule

Weekly Schedule Standards

  • Morning Drill: Every Morning (0600–0710)
  • Uniform Inspection: Every White Day & Friday (Service Khakis / NWU Type III)
  • In-Class PT: Every Wednesday & Thursday (Navy Blue/Gold PT Gear)
  • Raiders Practice: Black Days (1500–1700)

Plan of the Week

Cadets must maintain uniform readiness and bring required physical training gear on designated PT days. Attendance at morning drill practices is mandatory for competition team members.

Academic Expectations

Cadets are expected to maintain good academic standing across all high school coursework to remain eligible for competition teams and promotion.

Weekly knowledge checks evaluate core naval concepts, orders to the officer of the deck, rank recognition, and chain of command structure.

Four-Year Naval Science Curriculum

NS-1

Introduction to NJROTC

Foundations of Government, Navy Ships & Aviation, Military Customs, Drill, Fitness, First Aid.

NS-2

Naval Leadership & Nautical Sciences

Maritime Geography, Oceanography, Meteorology, Physics of Flight, Electricity & Electronics.

NS-3

Naval Knowledge & Skills

Sea Power, Naval Operations, Communications, Ship Construction, Navigation, Seamanship.

NS-4

Seminars in Practical Leadership

Cultural Awareness, SAT/ACT Prep, Suicide Prevention, Advanced Leadership.
