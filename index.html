Welcome to Grizzly Company

Mesa Ridge High School NJROTC develops informed and responsible citizens dedicated to character growth, leadership excellence, and service to our community and nation. Operating in Rooms 112 and 114, our unit upholds the highest traditions of naval leadership, teamwork, and scholastic dedication.

OUR MISSION

"To instill in each cadet an awareness of their responsibilities as United States citizens, to develop strong moral character, and to build skills for personal growth and leadership."

Naval Science Curriculum

The NJROTC curriculum is structured to build progressive leadership, nautical science expertise, and civic responsibility across four academic years:

Naval Science 1: Introduction

Focuses on introduction to the naval service, military law, basic citizenship, leadership foundations, and wellness/fitness conditioning.

Naval Science 2: Maritime History

Covers maritime history, naval heritage, leadership theory, communication skills, and introduction to navigation and seamanship.

Naval Science 3: Leadership & Strategies

Emphasizes advanced naval leadership, naval operations, meteorology, astronomy, physics, and practical command responsibility.

Naval Science 4: Senior Management

Practical application of cadet corps management, mentorship, financial responsibility, and advanced ethics.

The Eleven General Orders to the Sentry

Order General Order Statement
1 To take charge of this post and all government property in view.
2 To walk my post in a military manner, keeping always on the alert and observing everything that takes place within sight or hearing.
3 To report all violations of orders I am instructed to enforce.
4 To repeat all calls from posts more distant from the guardhouse than my own.
5 To quit my post only when properly relieved.
6 To receive, obey, and pass on to the sentry who relieves me, all orders from the Commanding Officer, Command Duty Officer, Officer of the Deck, and all officers and petty officers of the watch only.
7 To talk to no one except in the line of duty.
8 To give the alarm in case of fire or disorder.
9 To call the Officer of the Deck in any case not covered by instructions.
10 To salute all officers and all colors and standards not cased.
11 To be especially watchful at night and, during the time for challenging, to challenge all persons on or near my post, and to allow no one to pass without proper authority.
