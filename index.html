NJROTC Academics & Naval Science Curriculum

The NJROTC curriculum combines classroom instruction, practical leadership exercises, physical fitness, and naval history across four years of high school courses.

Naval Science 1 (NS1)

Introduction to NJROTC, citizenship, leadership, drill, uniform wear, customs and courtesies, and basic naval orientation.

Naval Science 2 (NS2)

Maritime history, nautical sciences, oceanography, meteorological study, and intermediate drill leadership.

Naval Science 3 (NS3)

Naval knowledge, sea power, national security, naval weapons, navigation fundamentals, and staff management.

Naval Science 4 (NS4)

Advanced leadership theory, unit command execution, mentorship of junior cadets, and career planning.

Competitive Teams

Armed & Unarmed Drill Team

Precision marching, regulation drill routines, and exhibition drill performances in regional competitions.

Color Guard Team

Presents the National Ensign and Navy Flag at school assemblies, games, and community ceremonies.

Academic Team

Competes in nautical science, history, current events, and STEM academic postal meets.

Physical Fitness (PT) Team

Trains for push-ups, plank endurance, and 1.5-mile athletic competitions.

Cadet Knowledge & Study Guide

Navy Orders to the Sentry

  1. Take charge of this post and all government property in view.
  2. Walk my post in a military manner, keeping always on the alert...
  3. Report all violations of orders I am instructed to enforce.
  4. Repeat all calls from posts more distant from the guard house than my own.
  5. Quit my post only when properly relieved.

Navy Enlisted Rank Structure

  • E-1: Seaman Recruit (SR)
  • E-2: Seaman Apprentice (SA)
  • E-3: Seaman (SN)
  • E-4: Petty Officer Third Class (PO3)
  • E-5: Petty Officer Second Class (PO2)
  • E-6: Petty Officer First Class (PO1)
