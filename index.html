Naval Science Curriculum

The NJROTC curriculum is structured to build progressive leadership, nautical science expertise, and civic responsibility across four academic years:

Naval Science 1: Introduction

Focuses on introduction to the naval service, military law, basic citizenship, leadership foundations, and wellness/fitness conditioning.

Naval Science 2: Maritime History

Covers maritime history, naval heritage, leadership theory, communication skills, and introduction to navigation and seamanship.

Naval Science 3: Leadership & Strategies

Emphasizes advanced naval leadership, naval operations, meteorology, astronomy, physics, and practical command responsibility.

Naval Science 4: Senior Management

Practical application of cadet corps management, mentorship, financial responsibility, and advanced ethics.

Competitive & Specialized Teams

Raiders Team

High-endurance physical conditioning team focusing on obstacle courses, rope bridges, ruck marches, and strength competitions.

Color Guard

Precision presentation of the American and Navy flags at school athletic events, community ceremonies, and official functions.

Drill Team

Exhibition and regulation armed and unarmed drill squads showcasing synchronized movements and military bearing.

Marksmanship Team

Air rifle safety, precision marksmanship training, and postal rifle competitions governed by strict safety standards.
