Welcome to Grizzly Company

The Mesa Ridge High School Navy JROTC program is dedicated to instilling principles of leadership, citizenship, discipline, and academic excellence. Grizzly Company cadets participate in rigorous physical training, community service, drill competitions, and leadership development.

Located in Rooms 112 and 114, our unit operates under the direction of Senior Naval Science Instructor LCDR Tidwell and Naval Science Instructor SCPO Pearson.

Teams & Drill Units

Raiders Athletic Team

Focuses on high-intensity functional fitness, endurance running, team physical events, and obstacle conditioning.

Drill Team

Precision armed and unarmed military drill team representing Grizzly Company in regional competitions.

Color Guard

Presents colors for athletic events, school assemblies, community ceremonies, and formal parades.

Marksmanship & Rifle Team

Trains cadets in safety, precision target shooting, concentration, and competitive marksmanship.
