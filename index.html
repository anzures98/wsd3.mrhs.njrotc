Unit Mission

Honor • Courage • Commitment

The mission of Mesa Ridge High School NJROTC Grizzly Company is to instill in students the values of citizenship, service to the United States, personal responsibility, and a sense of accomplishment. We mold tomorrow's leaders through discipline, teamwork, and academic excellence.

Cadet Core Operating Principles

  • Grooming standards must be strictly maintained per Navy regulations on inspection days.
  • Cadets are expected to render proper military courtesy and salutes to cadet officers and instructors.
  • PQS (Personnel Qualification Standards) sign-offs must be completed prior to promotion boards.
  • Unexcused absences from team practices affect competition team roster eligibility.

Four-Year Naval Science Curriculum

NS-1

Introduction to NJROTC

Foundations of Government, Navy Ships & Aviation, Military Customs, Drill, Fitness, First Aid.

NS-2

Naval Leadership & Nautical Sciences

Maritime Geography, Oceanography, Meteorology, Physics of Flight, Electricity & Electronics.

NS-3

Naval Knowledge & Skills

Sea Power, Naval Operations, Communications, Ship Construction, Navigation, Seamanship.

NS-4

Seminars in Practical Leadership

Cultural Awareness, SAT/ACT Prep, Suicide Prevention, Advanced Leadership.
